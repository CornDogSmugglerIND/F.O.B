Part 1 — Companies

Google Analytics (reselling angle)

  • Tracks traffic, sources, clicks, conversions
  • Helps sellers see what converts vs just gets views
  • Useful for storefront / marketplace decisions

Tableau

  • Turns data into dashboards/charts
  • Self-service BI / visual reports
  • Example: companies track CX / performance faster

Compare the two

  • GA = tracks online behavior
  • Tableau = visualizes / explores data
  • Same goal (better decisions), different tools

Part 2 — Jobs

Job 1: E-commerce Analyst (reselling angle)

  • Does: sales, traffic, pricing, inventory insights
  • Industries: retail, eBay/Amazon, collectibles
  • K: business/marketing know-how
  • S: Excel, Google Analytics, dashboards
  • A: spot trends, explain numbers, act fast

Job 2: Business Analyst

  • Does: bridge business + data, improve processes
  • Industries: finance, healthcare, tech, retail, gov
  • K: often bachelor’s in business/related
  • S: Excel, SQL, Tableau/Power BI
  • A: communication, problem-solving, organize

Wrap-up

Conclusion (3–5 sentences)

  • Tools help understand customers/performance
  • These jobs turn data into action
  • For online selling: better list / price / promote
